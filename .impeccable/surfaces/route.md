# Home

## Scope and mode

- `/` landing; shared masthead, footer, and auth modal.
- Mode: Persuade.

## Audience, job, action

- 전 연령의 자기계발 독자, 특히 젊은 층. 처음 방문해 30초 안에 서비스의 약속과 세 가지 계획의 구조를 이해하고 인터뷰를 시작한다.
- Primary action: `나의 플래너 시작하기`. Secondary: `시작하기` in the closing section.
- Proof: the three plan kinds with a 5-year chronology example, labeled as example. No invented testimonials or claims.

## Constraints

- Preserve factual copy, labels, routes, auth behavior, and accessible names.
- Tablet-first; mobile responsive.
- Avoid cold SaaS, hype, pretty-but-unclear, and interrogation. The user chose the editorial system family after three direction rounds; the built expression is a contemporary cultural quarterly. No literal place scene; typography and grid carry the world.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Ople reads as a quarterly of possible lives, not a productivity dashboard: a warm editorial system where the three plans and the five-year chronology are the content.
OWN-WORLD: warm paper (#f3efe6), ink (#1b1a17), mute (#6b675d), and three role colors — olive A, cobalt B, persimmon C — on a 12-column asymmetric grid; Pretendard display/UI, Song Myung for decks and pull quotes; hairline rules, large numerals, folios, no literal scene.
STORY: the visitor sees the offer, the three futures, and the working five-year mechanism in one viewport, then starts the voice interview.
FIRST VIEWPORT: masthead `Ople · Odyssey Planning · VOL. 01` and an issue line; left seven columns: huge headline `하나의 정답 대신, 세 가지 삶의 가능성`, serif deck, primary ink button, and a three-figure product fact row; right five columns: a contents panel with A/B/C color bars and micro chronologies, plus the `5 YEARS / 60 MONTHS` figure.
FORM: editorial system, the user's chosen family after rounds 1–3; seed: d1c95d0f2a671ff1 (the safer-register round that produced the selection).
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.
